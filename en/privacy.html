aiity - privacy

Privacy Policy

For sAiity and this website · Last updated: 17 August 2026

In short: sAiity has no account and no audio-upload service. Speech recognition, translation, dictation, and saved transcripts run on your Mac. The optional caption microphone mixer is off by default, and dictation opens the microphone only while the configured press-to-talk key is held.

1. Who is responsible, and contact

sAiity is a private project and part of the aiity family. Provider details and the contact address are published in the imprint on aiity.de.

2. No accounts, no tracking

There is no registration, login, analytics SDK, or advertising. sAiity does not send us audio recordings, transcripts, or usage profiles. The app checks for updates only when you enable that option.

3. Audio processing on the Mac

System audio is captured through ScreenCaptureKit and recognised locally. Translation and transcript storage stay on the device. Models are downloaded from their declared source on first use and then used locally.

4. Optional microphone access

The Include my microphone setting is off by default. If you enable it, your microphone is mixed into system-audio recognition so your side of a conversation can be included in the transcript.

Dictation is a separate press-to-talk mode. The microphone opens only while the configured dictation shortcut is held and is released afterwards. When possible, the result is inserted into the focused field; a copy always remains on the clipboard.

5. Saved transcripts and exports

Saving sessions is optional. Saved originals, translations, and exports remain in your local sAiity data store. You decide whether to share an exported file.

6. This website

This page is static. It sets no cookies and loads no external fonts, scripts, or analytics. The web server processes technically necessary connection data to deliver and secure the page.

7. Your rights and changes

Privacy questions can be sent to the address listed in the imprint. If sAiity's behavior changes, this policy is updated. The version published here, with the date shown above, is the one that applies.
