# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People on Apple Silicon Macs who want live captions, local translation, saved
transcripts, or press-to-talk dictation without an account or cloud audio
service. They arrive at this page to understand what sAiity does, verify the
privacy model, and install the current signed release.

## Product Purpose

sAiity turns speech from the Mac into captions, translations, transcripts, and
dictation while keeping processing on the device. The public site succeeds when
a visitor understands that promise quickly, sees the real product in use, and
can download the current Mac release without hunting through repository files.

## Brand Personality

Direct, private, precise. The voice should feel like a capable macOS utility:
calmly confident, specific about permissions and limits, and never louder than
the product needs to be.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble generic AI SaaS landing pages: no gradient hero, glowing blobs,
fading background, glassmorphism, testimonial wall, fake metrics, oversized
claims, or decorative dashboard cards. Do not imply that the implementation is
open source. Do not hide important requirements or privacy qualifications
behind vague marketing copy.

## Design Principles

- Show the product doing its job with real sAiity screenshots.
- Make the local-processing and microphone model easy to verify.
- Put the download path first and keep supporting links obvious.
- Use restraint as product confidence: every visual element must explain,
  demonstrate, or guide.
- Preserve the specificity of a native Mac tool instead of borrowing SaaS
  conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the static page. Keep body and link contrast readable in
both light and dark appearances, provide meaningful image alternatives, support
keyboard navigation and visible focus, preserve content at small widths, and
respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not make meaning depend on color or motion.
