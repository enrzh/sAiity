A few useful details.

Will it work on my Mac?

sAiity requires macOS 26 or later and Apple Silicon. Intel Macs and Windows are not supported releases. Model speed depends on your Mac, available memory and the models you choose.

Does it need the internet?

To download models and, if enabled, check for updates. Once the chosen models are installed, recognition and translation run locally.

Can I use more than one language?

Yes. Language coverage depends on the recognition and translation engines you select. Dictation without translation preserves supported spoken languages; turning translation on asks for the result in your chosen target language.

How do local summaries work?

Version 2.6.6 opens Summarize, Summarize Again and Remove Summary in the resizable transcript reader. It uses the Gemma cleanup model already installed for dictation and does not download another model. Summaries can miss or invent details, and long transcripts may omit earlier lines. Original spoken lines and subtitle exports are preserved.
